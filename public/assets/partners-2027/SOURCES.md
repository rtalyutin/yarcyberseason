# Partner-page original marks and illustrations — 14 October 2026

Game logos and partner marks are downloaded originals, never generated or traced.
Transparent marks retain the publisher's colors and proportions. Image encoding
or size derivatives do not alter the mark. Brand surfaces preserve contrast.

## Atmospheric artwork

The Dota city and CS2 header stage are existing project assets. Corporate retains
the actual existing Yaroslavl Strelka photograph and its shared credit.
Three standalone vertical discipline illustrations were generated with built-in
ImageGen from the selected UI reference, inspected and encoded to 800 × 1000
WebP. They are scene illustrations, not documentary event photographs:

- cs2-art.webp: blue industrial lighting, two tactical operators, no marks/text.
- tanks-art.webp: heavy tank and ruined stone city, muted warm daylight.
- tekken-art.webp: Jin character, red gloves/sparks, dark lower overlay region.

Logo layers are separate original files, never part of the generated artwork.
