# Homepage partner logos

Original brand artwork, retrieved 14 October 2026. The homepage uses the inverse
versions on a dark teal surface. Other partner consumers retain their current
assets and content; `homeLogoUrl`, `homeRole`, and `homeUrl` are homepage fields.

- `dodo-pizza-inverse.svg`: official Dodo Brands RGB archive,
  https://shift.dodobrands.io/cdn/Russian-RGB.zip, Russian One Line / Colour —
  White Letters. The original extracted SVG is unchanged.
- `torrefacto-inverse.svg`: the original logo used by Torrefacto's homepage,
  https://www.torrefacto.ru/local/templates/torrefacto_2024/assets/torrefacto-homepage/v8.1.2/images/tf-logo-inverse-horizontal.svg.
  The downloaded SVG is unchanged.
- `redragon.svg`: original inline SVG inside `a.rd-header__logotype` at
  https://redragon.ru/. Paths, colors and `viewBox="0 0 133 89"` are unchanged.
  Root width is normalized from 91 to 133 to match that viewBox and avoid
  letterboxing when used as an external image. Height stays 89.

No brand marks were drawn, generated, traced, recolored, or substituted with text.
Roles and partner membership come from the existing confirmed partner registry.
The homepage contact action stays `mailto:info@ycs.bar`; it sends nothing itself.
